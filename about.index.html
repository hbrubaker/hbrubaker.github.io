Meet Harry Brubaker | JNL 221 | Home

Harry Brubaker is a Syracuse University student at the S.I. Newhouse School of Public Communications, where he studies Magazine, News and Digital Journalism.

Originally from New Jersey, Brubaker is an emerging journalist with experience covering sports, politics, and community news. He has written for Paterson Press, NorthJersey.com, and the Montclair Local, reporting on topics ranging from local elections and community initiatives to professional soccer and the 2026 FIFA World Cup.

A passionate sports journalist, Brubaker also has an interest in soccer media and digital storytelling. He is continuing to develop his reporting, interviewing and multimedia skills while pursuing a career in journalism.
